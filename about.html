About Us

Solly Dessert Shop Store

Solly Dessert Store was founded on a simple belief: dessert is the final note of any occasion, and it should be unforgettable. With over 8 years of experience in pâtisserie, our head chef trained in classic French techniques and brings that precision to every South African favorite we create. We specialize in bespoke celebration cakes and luxury desserts for weddings, milestones, and corporate events across Gauteng. Each design is sketched, crafted, and decorated in-house using imported couverture chocolate, Madagascar vanilla, and seasonal local fruit. Our atelier operates by appointment to ensure every client receives undivided attention and every cake receives the time it deserves. We don’t mass produce. We create edible art. We look forward to designing something beautiful for you.
